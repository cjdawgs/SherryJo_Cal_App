Privacy Policy

SherryJo Cal App · Last updated: November 5, 2026

SherryJo Cal App ("the App") is a private calendar application that combines a user's own Google, Microsoft and Apple calendars into one view, including a TV display. This policy explains what information the App uses and how it is handled.

Information the App accesses

  • Account details: the email address and basic profile information of each calendar account you connect, used to identify the account.
  • Calendar data: events, calendars and related details (titles, times, locations, descriptions) from the accounts you connect.
  • Sign-in credentials for connected providers: OAuth access and refresh tokens (or, for Apple, an app-specific password you provide), which the App needs to keep your calendars in sync.
  • App data you create: events, notes, tasks and display preferences you enter in the App.

How the information is used

The information is used only to provide the App's features: showing your events, creating and editing events you ask the App to change, keeping connected calendars in sync, and displaying your schedule on a paired TV. The App does not use your data for advertising and does not sell it.

Google user data and Limited Use

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the user-facing calendar features described above. It is not transferred to others except as needed to operate the App, to comply with law, or with your consent. It is not used for advertising, and humans do not read it except with your consent, for security purposes, or to comply with law.

Storage and security

Data is stored in a managed PostgreSQL database. Provider tokens are encrypted before storage. The App runs on Cloudflare Workers and Render, which process data on the App's behalf to deliver the service. Data is transmitted over HTTPS.

Sharing

Your data is not shared with third parties other than the infrastructure providers above and the calendar providers you choose to connect (Google, Microsoft, Apple), which receive only the changes you make.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account is connected. You can disconnect any calendar account at any time from the Accounts page, which removes its stored tokens and synced events from the App. You can also revoke the App's access from your Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or your Microsoft account. To request deletion of all of your data, contact us at the address below.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13.
