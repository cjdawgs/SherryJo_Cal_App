SherryJo Cal App

SherryJo Cal App is a private calendar application that brings your Google, Microsoft and Apple calendars together in one place and shows your schedule on a TV display.

What the app does

  • Shows events from all of your connected calendar accounts in a single month, week and day view.
  • Lets you create and edit events and publishes the changes back to the calendar you choose.
  • Keeps your calendars in sync automatically in the background.
  • Displays your upcoming schedule on a paired TV for the whole household.

Why it asks for Google Calendar access

When you connect a Google account, the app asks for permission to view and edit your Google Calendar events (the calendar scope) and to read your email address and basic profile so it can identify the account. This access is used only to show your events, apply the changes you make, and keep calendars in sync. You can disconnect an account or revoke access at any time. See the Privacy Policy for details, including how Google user data is handled under the Google API Services User Data Policy Limited Use requirements.
